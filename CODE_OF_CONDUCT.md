# Community standards

We want this project to be a respectful and useful place for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be considerate, patient, specific, and open to correction. Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intimidation, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

To report a conduct concern, contact a project maintainer privately through the repository's contact options. Do not publish personal information or sensitive incident details in a public issue.
