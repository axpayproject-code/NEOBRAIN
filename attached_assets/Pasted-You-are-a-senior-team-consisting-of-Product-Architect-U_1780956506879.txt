Product Overview

NEOBRAIN is a multi-tenant developmental intelligence platform serving four customer types:

1. Families (B2C Subscription)

Parents track children’s developmental progress, complete assessments, receive AI-generated insights, manage therapy plans, communicate with specialists, and coordinate care.

2. Clinics (Clinic SaaS)

Doctors, psychologists, therapists, and staff manage patients, review developmental timelines, conduct assessments, triage cases, monitor therapy, and generate reports.

3. Schools (School License)

Teachers, SPED coordinators, principals, and administrators monitor student development, submit observations, manage interventions, coordinate with parents, and generate school reports.

4. Government (LGU / Public Health)

Barangays, RHUs, LGUs, provincial offices, and national agencies monitor anonymized population-level developmental trends and resource planning.

⸻

PRIMARY DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The platform must:

* Maintain a longitudinal timeline.
* Be consent-driven.
* Be non-diagnostic.
* Support role-based access.
* Support web and mobile responsive interfaces.
* Support multi-tenancy.
* Support future AI integrations.
