# ACCENTECX AI CARE (FULL PLATFORM SPECIFICATION)

## National AI-Assisted Developmental Healthcare Infrastructure

---

# 1. PRODUCT DEFINITION (FINAL FORM)

## What it is

A nationwide, multi-layer SaaS healthcare infrastructure that connects:

* Parents
* Children
* Schools
* Therapists
* Clinics
* Hospitals
* Government units

into a single **continuous developmental intelligence system**.

---

## Core Function

The system performs 5 continuous functions:

1. Early developmental screening
2. Behavioral data collection (questionnaires + video)
3. AI-assisted clinical structuring (not diagnosis)
4. Specialist coordination + telehealth
5. Long-term developmental tracking

---

## What it is NOT

* NOT a diagnosis system
* NOT a replacement for doctors
* NOT a chatbot
* NOT a simple telemedicine app

---
