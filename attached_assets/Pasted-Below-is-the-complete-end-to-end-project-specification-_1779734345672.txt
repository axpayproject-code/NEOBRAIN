# ACCENTECX AI CARE

## FULL PRODUCT SPECIFICATION (END-TO-END SYSTEM)

---

# 1. PRODUCT DEFINITION

## Name

ACCENTECX AI CARE

## Type

Multi-tenant SaaS + AI clinical decision-support + developmental care coordination system

## Core Mission

To detect, structure, and manage child developmental concerns through:

* standardized screening
* behavioral video analysis
* AI-assisted clinical structuring
* specialist coordination
* long-term developmental tracking

---

## Target Conditions Supported (NOT DIAGNOSED)

* Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
* ADHD
* Speech Delay
* Learning Disabilities
* Developmental Delay
* Behavioral Regulation Issues

---

## Critical Rule

The system NEVER diagnoses.
It only produces:

> structured developmental risk indicators + referral recommendations

## STEP 2 — Child Profile Creation

* age
* language
* developmental concerns
* medical history

---

## STEP 3 — Structured Screening

Adaptive questionnaire system:

* communication
* social behavior
* attention
* motor skills
* emotional regulation
* sensory behavior

---

## STEP 4 — VIDEO ASSESSMENT

Guided structured tasks:

1. Name response test
2. Joint attention test
3. Social interaction play
4. Communication prompts
5. Free behavior observation

---

## STEP 5 — AI PROCESSING

System generates:

### 1. Domain Scores

* Communication (0–100)
* Social Interaction (0–100)
* Attention (0–100)
* Motor Skills (0–100)
* Emotional Regulation (0–100)

---

### 2. Behavioral Pattern Clusters

* speech delay patterns
* ADHD-like behavior patterns
* ASD behavioral indicators
* sensory processing patterns

---

### 3. Risk Classification

* Low
* Moderate
* High
* Critical

## STEP 6 — REPORT GENERATION
