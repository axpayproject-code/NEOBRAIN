THE SECRET

The parent should never open NEOBRAIN and see:

No new updates

Instead there should always be at least one of:

* Today’s mission
* New insight
* Weekly challenge
* Teacher update
* Therapy task
* New milestone
* New lesson
* Monthly report
* Achievement unlock

If that happens, NEOBRAIN stops being a screening platform and becomes a development engagement platform, which is what keeps families subscribed for 12+ months.
